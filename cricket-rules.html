Cricket Rules

1. Teams

Two teams play against each other. Each team has 11 players in a standard match.

2. Batting

The batting team tries to score runs by hitting the ball and running between wickets.

3. Bowling

The bowling team tries to dismiss the batsmen and prevent runs.

4. Overs

An over usually contains six valid deliveries bowled by one bowler.

5. Boundaries

A ball reaching the boundary after bouncing scores four runs. A ball clearing it scores six.

6. Wickets

A batsman can get out through bowled, caught, run out, LBW and stumped.
